UNIVERSITY RESEARCH PROJECT

Investigating Cybersecurity Attacks on Simulated Biological Neural Signal Processing

Final-year Computer Science dissertation at Oxford Brookes University.

Project Overview

This project investigates cybersecurity vulnerabilities in simulated biological neural signal processing systems. Using Unity, the research explores how malicious interference with neural signals could affect the behaviour of a simulated biological circuit.

Research Objectives

Develop a simulation of a biological neural circuit, establish baseline behaviour, and investigate the effects of simulated cybersecurity attacks.

Technologies

Unity C# Cybersecurity Simulation

Project Status

Ongoing — Research and development.
